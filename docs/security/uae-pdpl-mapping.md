# UAE PDPL mapping (GOV-5)

How Zelvo supports the obligations of **Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data** (PDPL) for the organisations that use it as controller. Article references follow the published law; the Executive Regulations may add detail (for example deadlines), so the configurable values below should be checked against them. Government entities are largely outside the federal PDPL and follow their emirate's data law; the same features serve those rules. Last reviewed with task L1 (Phase 2 release readiness, 2026-11-28).

| PDPL topic | Article (indicative) | Zelvo support | Configured by | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lawful processing and consent | Art. 4–6 | Versioned bilingual privacy notice accepted at first sign-in and on each new version, and by visitors before chat or anonymous messages; optional purposes granted and withdrawn with an unchangeable history | Settings → Privacy | `docs/architecture/privacy.md` |
| Controller's obligations, data minimisation | Art. 5, 7 | Personal data kept apart and masked (audit log, AI, integration log, telemetry); fields hidden unless a role is given access; anonymous complaints without personal details. Phase 2: web push messages carry only the event's title and the reference number, never names or ticket text (J2); the AI for plain-language rules gets services, categories and groups by reference and no people (K1); the what-if explanation gets figures and names of services, departments and changes, never people's names (H5); CMDB attributes hold no personal data fields (G1) | Roles, field rules, Settings → Notifications | `docs/architecture/identity-access.md`, `docs/architecture/customer-voice.md`, `docs/architecture/notifications.md` (Web push), `docs/architecture/changes.md` |
| Storage limitation | Art. 5 | Retention periods per record type and for unused accounts; records anonymised when they end; logs and temporary files cleared on schedule | Settings → Privacy | `docs/architecture/privacy.md` |
| Security of processing | Art. 20 | Row-level tenant isolation, encryption of stored secrets, TLS, mandatory second factor for staff, tamper-evident audit log; passkeys with user verification on the person's own device, so fingerprints and faces never reach Zelvo (J3); per-type permissions for custom record types and administrator approval of imported app packages (K2, K3) | Operator, administrators | `docs/security/uae-ia-mapping.md`, `docs/architecture/identity-access.md` (Passkeys) |
| Data breach notification | Art. 9 | Evidence from the audit log and telemetry; the notification to the UAE Data Office and to people is the organisation's process | Customer | — |
| Data protection officer | Art. 10–12 | The `privacy.requests` permission gives a DPO or privacy officer the requests queue | Roles | `docs/architecture/privacy.md` |

| Personal data on people's devices | Art. 20 | The installable web app keeps only the requester's own last-viewed tickets and unsent messages on the device, emptied for another person, after sign-out and when the tenant turns offline data off (J1); push devices are removed at sign-out on the device (J2) | Settings → Branding → Installable app | `docs/architecture/installable-app.md` |

| Cross-border transfer | Art. 22–23 | Self-hosted in the UAE (GOV-4); AI calls go only to the provider the customer chooses, with personal data masked | Operator, Settings → AI | `docs/architecture/ai-gateway.md` |
| Records of processing | Art. 7 | Audit log of every create, change, admin action and view of personal data | — | `docs/architecture/data-tenancy-audit.md` |
| Employee monitoring and workload data (K4) | Art. 4–7, 13–15 | Agent wellbeing off by default; turned on only after the administrator confirms staff were told, every agent notified; daily figures from existing Zelvo activity only, kept 90 days; individuals only for their team leads, totals for executives; every view audited; in the person's copy and erased with them; not for ranking (no league tables, exports or scheduled reports); never presence or keystrokes; not available to integrations; risks and measures in the impact note | Settings → Wellbeing | `docs/architecture/wellbeing.md`, `docs/security/wellbeing-dpia.md` |

Deadline for requests: 30 days by default (Settings → Privacy, 1–90 days).
